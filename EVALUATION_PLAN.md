# Evaluation Plan

## Problem Grounding

### 1. Who specifically has the collaboration problem?

- Researchers in academic research teams, especially lab members who collaboratively conduct literature reviews but read different sets of papers.

### 2. What do they currently do instead of our tool?

- Team members currently read papers individually and share their findings through meetings, messaging platforms, shared documents, or verbal discussions. They often need to manually summarize and combine information from different papers.

### 3. What would be observably different if our tool worked?

- Team members would be able to understand findings from papers read by other members more quickly and develop a more consistent shared understanding of the research field with less repeated explanation and discussion.

## Evaluation Plan

### Success Definition

- We will know our tool works if researchers can use it to understand and synthesize findings from papers read by different team members more efficiently and accurately than their current collaboration process.

- The AI will help organize and synthesize information from different papers, while researchers will review the synthesis and make the final judgment about its correctness and relevance.

### Target Users

- Graduate students and senior lab members who regularly read and discuss academic papers as part of their research.

- We plan to recruit participants from our university research labs.

### Method

- We will use structured observation and brief interviews.

- We will prepare six different sets of research papers, with three papers in each set. Using multiple paper sets helps reduce the risk that our results are affected by a single set of papers being unusually difficult or easy.

- Participants will complete literature synthesis tasks both with and without our tool. We will compare their performance under the two conditions, including the time needed to complete the task, the quality of the synthesis, and their understanding of the papers read by other team members.

- After the tasks, we will conduct brief interviews to collect participants' feedback on whether the tool helped them build a shared understanding of the literature more efficiently.
