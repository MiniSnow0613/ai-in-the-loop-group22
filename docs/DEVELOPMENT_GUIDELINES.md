## Development Guidelines

### 1. AI Usage and Verification

Every PR must explicitly describe what was generated or modified by AI and how the developer verified the AI-generated changes.

### 2. Branch Naming and Management

- Branch names must clearly reflect the purpose of the changes.
- Use descriptive prefixes such as `feat/`, `fix/`, `docs/`, or `refactor/`.
- Create a new branch for each distinct development task.
- Do not reuse branches for unrelated tasks.
- Delete branches after their corresponding PRs have been merged.

Examples:

- `feat/task-dependency-visualization`
- `fix/off-topic-input`
- `docs/update-development-guidelines`
